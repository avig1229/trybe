# 🌿 Trybe Product Testing Guide

Welcome to the Trybe testing phase! Trybe is currently in its first phase: **Project Valley**—a utility focused on helping you organize your project material and maintain a consistent creative rhythm.

---

## 🚀 The Onboarding Flow (Day 1)

1.  **Welcome**: Log in via **Email** or **Google**.
2.  **Introduction**: Follow the guided introduction to understand the core philosophy of "Process Over Product."
3.  **The First Step**: You will encounter the **LoCommit** prompt immediately. To begin, follow the instructions to **Create your first project**—the project you are currently working on in your creative life.
4.  **Unlock the Valley**: Once your project is created, upload your **daily progress** (an image, a piece of code, a sketch, or a quick note). 
    *   *Note: This first upload is required to unlock your full page navigation for the day.*
5.  **Explore**: Spend a few minutes navigating between your new project and the dashboard. 
    *   *💡 Action: Please jot down your first impressions on how easy it is to navigate the page—does the layout feel intuitive or confusing after the unlock?*

---

## 🏃 The 5-Day Commitment Challenge
This test is designed to see if Trybe helps you stay accountable to your own process.

### 📅 Day 2: Starting the Streak
- **Goal**: Establish consistency and test organization.
- **Action**: Return to the app. You will be locked again! Upload today's progress to start your **Streak**. 
- **Deep Dive**: Now that you have a second day of work, try adding **Channels** (sub-folders) and different **Blocks** (text, images, or videos) to organize your project material.

### 📅 Day 3-4: Build Your Catalog
- **Goal**: Test the "Infinite Shelf" of your project.
- **Action**: Continue your daily uploads. Try to move beyond the surface level—add reference images, research notes, and mood boards into different channels.

### 📅 Day 5: The Reflection
- **Goal**: Evaluate the experience.
- **Action**: Perform your final upload for the week. Review your "Valley" and see how your project has grown over just 5 days.

## 📝 User Feedback & Survey
Once you have completed your 5-day test, we would love your honest feedback. Please reflect on these questions:

### 💡 Intuitiveness & Usability
1.  **The Onboarding**: Did the sequence of *Login -> Intro -> Create Project -> Upload* feel natural, or was it a hurdle?
2.  **Organization**: Does the *Projects -> Channels -> Blocks* structure make sense for organizing your specific creative work?

### 🔄 Retention
1.  **Daily Habit**: Did the "Daily Lock" help remind you to document your work, or did it feel intrusive?
2.  **Continued Use**: If the platform were live, would you use it as your primary place to collect and organize project materials? Why or why not?

### 🌍 The Future: "Creator BeReal"
1.  **Community Concept**: We are considering building this into a community-based platform—think of it as a **"Creator BeReal"** where you see the raw, daily progress of other creators. Does this sound like a platform that would be valuable for the creators around you?
2.  **Creative Belonging**: Would seeing others' messy progress (not just their finished portfolio) make you feel more connected to a creative community?
