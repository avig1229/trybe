The Problem

Why current tools fail creators
01 · Tools

Fragmentation

Creators scatter work across Pinterest, Notion, Figma, and Slack. No single workspace unifies the creative lifecycle.

02 · Social

Creative Isolation

Solo creators lack daily standups or studio critiques. No forcing function for consistency.

03 · Culture

Survivorship Bias

Platforms like Behance reward only polished finals. The messy process — where growth happens — stays invisible.

"Existing tools force you to choose between private productivity and public performance. Trybe unifies them."
